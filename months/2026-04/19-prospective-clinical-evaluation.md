# Prospective clinical evaluation

Status: draft — expansion and review pending
Sources: [Google DeepMind — 2026-04-30](https://deepmind.google/blog/ai-co-clinician/)

## In one sentence

Prospective clinical evaluation tests an AI-supported workflow in the intended setting and population before claims about care quality or safety are generalized.

## Draft lesson

Retrospective datasets and simulations are valuable but cannot reproduce real handoffs, missing records, time pressure, patient consent, and clinician disagreement. A prospective protocol defines inclusion criteria, outcome measures, safety escalation, data governance, monitoring, and stop conditions before enrolling cases.

The April co-clinician announcement describes research and a simulation study. Treat that as evidence about the reported experiment, not authorization to deploy. For an early pilot, keep a clinician in control, preserve source evidence, log suggestion acceptance and correction, and investigate harmful or near-miss output with clinical governance.
