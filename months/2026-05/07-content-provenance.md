# Content provenance
Status: draft — expansion and review pending
Sources: [Google DeepMind — AI-powered pointing](https://deepmind.google/blog/ai-pointer/)

## Draft lesson
Content provenance records where an artifact came from and how it changed. It can provide tamper evidence and process history, but it does not prove the content is true or that every edit was captured. Store signed manifests, parent IDs, transformation version, and retention policy; show users uncertainty when provenance is missing.
